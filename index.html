GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Answers to common questions about discussing a potential investigation.

How do I know if my situation is a fit?

Start with a brief initial conversation about what you need to learn. The investigator can explain whether your request is within the services actually offered and what lawful options may be available.

Do I need to have evidence before getting started?

Not necessarily. A clear explanation of the concern and any facts you already know may be enough for an initial case assessment. Avoid sending highly sensitive personal information through an unverified website contact form.

How much do private investigation services cost?

Costs depend on the type of assignment, scope, research required, and time involved. Actual pricing, retainers, and payment policies should be confirmed directly with the business owner.

What areas can you serve?

The website concept focuses on Phoenix and neighboring Valley communities. The actual service area should be verified with the owner, including whether any particular case can be accepted.
